How the score works: each indicator is scored from −100 (strongly bearish) to +100 (strongly bullish) from its latest reading, weighted by importance and by how much it applies to each asset (oil, for example, lifts gold but weighs on copper), then averaged. It describes the pressure on prices, not where they will go. Central bank data arrives with a one to two month lag. This is a research aid, not financial advice.
